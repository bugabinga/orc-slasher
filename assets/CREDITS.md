## assets/custom/ — original art for this project (CC0)

Hand-made pixel sprites generated by `tools/make_custom_art.py`:

- `orc_warchief_*` — original boss sprite (horned iron helm, warpaint, glowing eyes)
- `orc_berserker_*` — red elite recolor derived from 0x72's orc_warrior (CC0 base)
- `campfire_f0..3` — animated campfire for the between-wave rest scene
- `xp_gem_*`, `shaman_bolt_*`, `slash_f0..2`, `card` — pickups, projectiles, FX, UI

Also released as CC0.

## assets/custom/hero_* , gun_* , proj_* — gunner heroes & guns (CC0)

Generated by `tools/make_gun_art.py`: nine heroes on one shared body
template (helmet / hat / gas mask / balaclava headgear + palettes), twelve
side-view guns, the rocket and the grenade shell. Original, CC0.

## assets/custom/city_* — abandoned city tiles & props (CC0)

Generated by `tools/make_city_art.py`: asphalt, crosswalks, sidewalks,
lots, grass, plaza bricks, roofs, brick/concrete/beige facades (dark, lit,
smashed and boarded windows; doors), wrecked cars in six colours and two
orientations, streetlights, barrels, dumpster, trash, barricade, manhole,
decals and a dead tree. Original, CC0.

## assets/custom/city_k_* — Kenney "Roguelike Modern City" (CC0)

Street furniture and junk cut from Kenney's pack
(https://opengameart.org/content/roguelike-modern-city-pack, CC0) by
`tools/extract_kenney.py`, desaturated and darkened to fit the dead city.

## assets/zombies/ — zombified enemy frames

`tools/zombify.py` recolors every orc, goblin and ogre frame above (skin to
corpse grey-green, rags desaturated, deep shadows to dried blood). Each file
keeps its source name and replaces it at build time. Licenses follow the
source frame: 0x72 / original art stays CC0, CraftPix-derived frames stay
under the CraftPix license.

## assets/sounds/ — CC0 sound effects

Encoded to small mono MP3s by `tools/build_sounds.py` from:

- **Zombies Sound Pack** by artisticdude (CC0) —
  https://opengameart.org/content/zombies-sound-pack → `groan_*`, `zdie_*`, `zboss`
- **Residue SFX** by Planet Killer Games (CC0; "visit my website":
  http://www.planetkillergames.net/) —
  https://opengameart.org/content/residue-sfx → `boom`, `clip`, `spit`, `heal`

Gunshots and UI beeps are still synthesized live (WebAudio).
